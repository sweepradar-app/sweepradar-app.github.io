SweepRadar

A private sweepstakes organizer for Google Chrome.

SweepRadar is a Chrome extension that helps a small group of people keep their sweepstakes organized: saving sweepstakes they want to enter, opening them on a schedule, and keeping track of entries, wins and expenses.

SweepRadar is not sold or offered to the public. It is shared privately with a small group of invited users, and it is not available in the Chrome Web Store.

Google sign-in: SweepRadar can optionally connect to the user's own Google account to save backups to their own Google Drive and to read and update Google Sheets the user chooses. See the Privacy Policy for details.
