SweepRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

SweepRadar is a private Chrome extension for organizing sweepstakes. It is shared only with a small group of invited users and is not sold to the public. This page explains what information SweepRadar uses and how.

Information stored on your computer

The sweepstakes you save, your schedules, entries, wins, expenses and settings are stored in your own Chrome browser on your own computer. They are not sent to the developer or to any server run by SweepRadar. SweepRadar has no server of its own.

Google account access (optional)

If you choose to connect your Google account, SweepRadar asks Google for permission to:

  • Google Drive (only files SweepRadar creates or that you open with it): to save and restore backup copies of your SweepRadar data in your own Google Drive.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update the Google Sheets you choose to connect, such as a sheet of analyzed sweepstakes rules, a tag list, or a sheet of your wins and expenses.

This information goes only between your Chrome browser and your own Google account. The developer never receives it, does not store it, and cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking.
  • We do not use your Google data to train any artificial intelligence or machine learning model.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SweepRadar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Websites you visit

SweepRadar opens sweepstakes websites that you saved. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them.

Removing your information

You can delete any of your SweepRadar data inside the extension, or remove the extension from Chrome to delete everything stored on your computer. Backups in your Google Drive and your Google Sheets stay in your Google account until you delete them. You can remove SweepRadar's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

SweepRadar is shared privately. If you have a question about this policy, please contact the person who invited you to use SweepRadar.
